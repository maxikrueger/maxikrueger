## Projects

| Project | What it does | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| [**document-rag**](https://github.com/maxikrueger/document-rag) | Search assistant for document collections such as quality management systems. | [**news-agent**](https://github.com/maxikrueger/news-agent) | Personalised AI news digests from your own RSS feeds, delivered on a schedule to Discord or e-mail | FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Next.js · LLM summarisation |

| [**workforce-scheduling**](https://github.com/maxikrueger/workforce-scheduling) | Assigns tasks to employees with a constraint solver and refines the plan with employee feedback (bachelor's thesis) | Python · OptaPy · Tkinter · SQLite |
